We propose a **prior knowledge-aware fusion network** for early prediction of macrovascular invasion (MVI) in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) patients. The model fuses liver CT imaging and clinical indicators, introducing **word vector encoding** to enhance representation of clinical features. Assists clinicians in treatment decision-making and reduces the risk of over-treatment.
