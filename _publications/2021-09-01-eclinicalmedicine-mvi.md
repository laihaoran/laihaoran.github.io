We propose a **CT-imaging-only prediction framework** for future macrovascular invasion in hepatocellular carcinoma. Using **graph convolutional networks** to capture tumor boundary features combined with global liver structural information, this approach achieves high-precision early prediction without relying on clinical indicators.
